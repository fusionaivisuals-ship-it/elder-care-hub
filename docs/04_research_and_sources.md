# 04: Research and sources (verified 2026-10-17)

## Official rules (from 1966.gov.tw and MOHW)
- **Eligibility:**
  - The city LTC management center assesses the person at **level 2+**, AND one of: 65+ · Indigenous 55+ · dementia aged 50+ (49 and under added 2026-01-01) · disability with care needs · NHI post-acute care program (added 2026-01-01).
  - Source: https://1966.gov.tw/LTC/cp-6533-70777-207.html
- **Process:**
  1. Apply (1966 / city center / hospital discharge planning / online).
  2. Home assessment.
  3. Care plan with the A-unit case manager.
  4. Services from contracted providers.
- **Benefits are not reduced by income; only the copay changes** (official page).
- **Caps:**
  - care & professional per month by level: 10,020 / 15,460 / 18,580 / 24,100 / 28,070 / 32,090 / 36,180;
  - transport NT$1,680–2,400/month by zone;
  - devices NT$40,000 (group 1) or NT$60,000 (group 2, from 2026-07-01) per 3 years;
  - respite NT$32,340 (levels 2–6) / NT$48,510 (levels 7–8) per year.
- **Copay categories (regulation Art. 14):**
  - Type 1 = low-income OR mid-low-income households (0%).
  - Type 2 = low-middle-income senior allowance (1.5–2.5× tier) or disability living allowance (5–10%).
  - Type 3 = general (16–30%).
  - Rates: care/respite 16/5/0 · devices 30/10/0 · transport by zone 30/27/25/21 and 10/9/8/7.
- **Carry-over:**
  - Care and transport quotas run in 6-month periods from the plan approval month; unused amounts reset after that.
  - Pots can't be moved between each other.
  - Sources: regulation Art. 12; MOHW ruling 衛部顧字第1141960552號, https://laws.gov.taipei/Law/Interpretation/Content/FE388708
- **Devices:**
  - One amount per 3-year period counted from the care plan approval date; the group can't change within the period.
  - People who got group 1 on or before 2026-06-30 wait 3 years from that approval before group 2.
  - No re-subsidy within an item's minimum service life (also across the disability device subsidy).
  - The vendor bills the government directly, so you pay only the copay (since 2024-01-01).
  - Sources: https://1966.gov.tw/LTC/cp-6453-69940-207.html and the Q&A https://www.mohw.gov.tw/dl-101314-a5590920-5f8d-4ead-b508-fe4b204fcd36.html
- **Professional services:** max 12 sessions per goal within 6 months; a 90-day gap before a new goal under the same code. https://1966.gov.tw/LTC/cp-6451-70123-207.html
- **Respite prices:**
  - day care: full day 1,250 / half day 625;
  - residential 2,310 per 24h;
  - small multi-function center overnight 2,000;
  - neighborhood station 170/h;
  - in-home 770 per 2h.
  - Requires a family caregiver. https://1966.gov.tw/LTC/cp-6454-70075-207.html
- **Residential subsidy:**
  - NT$15,000/month (NT$180,000/year) for level 4+ or a moderate+ disability certificate; NT$5,000 for residents since before 2023-01-01.
  - A month counts only with half the month stayed.
  - Can't be combined in the same month with the disability residential subsidy, public placement or a veterans' home public bed.
  - Applications opened 2026-09-14.
  - Sources: https://1966.gov.tw/LTC/cp-6457-69925-207.html, Q&A https://www.mohw.gov.tw/dl-102600-e2676cd5-c570-41fd-8f29-0e1921ca8efe.html
- **Discharge planning:** 242 hospitals; the average time from discharge to services fell from 51 days to 4 days (target 2 days in 2026). https://1966.gov.tw/LTC/cp-6458-69942-207.html
- **Tax deduction:** NT$180,000 per person per year from 2025. https://1966.gov.tw/LTC/cp-6457-69928-207.html
- **City centers:**
  - Taipei 02-2537-1099, online form https://ltc.health.gov.taipei/tplcPublic/front/onlineService (asks for ID number, contact details, medical status, Indigenous status, family contacts; hotline hours Mon–Fri 08:30–12:00, 13:30–17:30).
  - New Taipei 02-2968-3331, https://service.ntpc.gov.tw/eservice/Ac124014.action
  - Source: https://1966.gov.tw/LTC/cp-6443-69944-207.html
- **Regulation texts:**
  - 2025 amendment: https://www.mohw.gov.tw/dl-95450-0fdec091-da73-42eb-9828-b897fac2a165.html
  - full-text mirror: https://laws.gov.taipei/Law/File/0000307475

## Pitch statistics
- 65+ share of Taiwan: **20.70%** (4,806,053), end of Sep 2026. Super-aged since Dec 2025 (20.06%). CNA: https://www.cna.com.tw/news/aipl/202610080068.aspx · https://www.cna.com.tw/news/ahel/202601090098.aspx
- Taipei 65+: **24.18%**, the highest in Taiwan. Wanhua **26.43%**. https://www.storm.mg/lifestyle/11167443
- LTC coverage: national 84.86% (2024); **Taipei 58.10%, 20th nationally**. MOHW: https://www.mohw.gov.tw/cp-17-88097-1.html
- LTC users: 757,089 in 2024. People needing LTC: 922,900 projected for 2026 and 1,295,900 for 2035 (LTC 3.0 plan). https://www.mohw.gov.tw/dl-98526-31ea7422-1b07-414b-a2a4-51e38c070ee4.html
- Respite users: 191,206 in 2024.
- Dementia: about 400,000 people; 490,000+ by 2031.
